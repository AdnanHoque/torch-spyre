TFLOP/s counts the logical matmul FLOPs (`2*M*K*N`) and uses the mean Kineto device-kernel duration. The scaled-FP8 kernel includes on-device FP16-to-FP8 Qfp8 conversion, relayout, FP8 matmul, and two scale-recovery stages. It uses fixed unit-valued per-row activation scales and per-output-channel weight scales; scale derivation and activation normalization are not included.
