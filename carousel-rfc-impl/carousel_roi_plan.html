Disposition · one card per carousel

Shelve

Weight Carousel

Rotating-operand W-carousel to cut prefill DRAM weight replication and make prefill compute-bound.

Why shelve: the entire problem framing is refuted at the root — replication is ≤8× not 32× modeled, the "30× under roofline" gap was a layout artifact already closed to ~73% util measured by a non-carousel fix, and the carousel changes only DRAM bytes — a saving invisible to the planner with no cost term to reward it. At production S≤512 the compute-bound gate is a roofline NO-GO modeled.

HarvestThe 130 GB/s uniform-shift transport finding measured — move it into the co-bundling / ring-fold work (moves 3–4), its value here is only ~1.7% of the layer.
DropThe ×32 replication headline, the "#2407 prunes token-split" claim, A3 channel-affinity, and the "prefill becomes compute-bound" claim at S≤512.
Reshape

KV Carousel

Sequence-sharded decode attention with a deterministic LSE ring-fold for long-context.

Why reshape: the structural idea and its numerics are sound, but every speed premise is refuted or unmeasured — the 4× ceiling depends on blocked channel affinity, the "fast transport" probe validated the wrong movement pattern, the fold needs an absent primitive behind a closed barrier, and the flash lowering is a stub. The decode-projection goal is already won cheaply by split-K + the cost-model fix, so the real remaining target shrinks to attention-over-cache.

KeepSeq-shard + LSE-fold + long-context capacity thesis (numerics de-risked 12/12 measured); it is independent of the collapsed bandwidth story.
ReplaceThe channel-affinity 4× ceiling → a measured core-ownership fill factor u; converge the fold onto the same cross-bundle co-bundling redesign as the all-gather (fund one vehicle, not two).

Land the two banked inductor levers, then spend the ring finding on co-bundling.

Together they already reach the prefill/matmul array-fill goal the weight carousel chased, plus most of the decode-projection goal the KV carousel targets — at near-zero cost.
