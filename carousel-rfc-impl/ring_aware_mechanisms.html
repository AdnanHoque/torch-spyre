The physics (treat as fixed)

Per-link transfer count sets bandwidth — not burst size

Fast band — nearest-neighbor pipelines, uniform rotations, reduce-scatter, multicast forwarding Contention floor — anything piling many transfers on one link Enlarging bursts 50–100× did nothing — count, not size.

The matmul cost model prices every move at flat bytes/128 — no contention, hop-distance, or multicast term. It cannot tell these two pictures apart, so it will never select a green plan over a red one of equal bytes. Selectability is a first-class requirement, not an afterthought.

BET 1 · THE ONE THING · flash-paged

Flash-in-a-loop: online-softmax as a coarse-tiled Lk reduction

inductor-only core prototype-now intel HIGH · prod HIGH

The only unmet goal with zero banked coverage. Today test_flash.py:18 forces kv_block_size=Lk, so the streaming loop runs once and the full [Lq,Lk] scores (32 MB/head modeled, ~12 ms HBM round-trip) materialize and spill — defeating flash. Stream Lk as a Stage-1 reduction keeping only O(Lq) running (m,l,A) state. Selectable today: the win is deleting real HBM bytes the flat pricer already counts, and flash is hint-driven. It is the mandatory substrate under the entire flash ring family.

First step

Adopt paged's scores.transpose(-1,-2) off-stick pattern, set kv_block_size<Lk; compile test_flash.py and confirm via TORCH_COMPILE_DEBUG that full scores no longer materialize and the reduction lands off-stick (no Stage-2 RuntimeError) — before touching the compound combine.

BET 2 · THE LINCHPIN · cross-cutting

Per-link-transfer-count contention cost term (+ cohort-penalty fix)

inductor-only prototype-now intel HIGH · prod MED

Without it no ring plan is cost-selectable — flat bytes/128 rates a fast uniform shift identical to a slow scatter. Sharpest live slice: work_division.py:684 charges a linear cohort_penalty=max(1,max(m,n)/8) to every broadcast, mis-modeling the proven multicast STCDPOpLx as a 36 GB/s scatter — actively suppressing the wide-N splits the banked array-fill / min-cores fixes want, and the attn all-gather past cohort 8 that flash needs.

First step

Gate cohort_penalty on identical-operand (multicast: ~const+hops) vs distinct-data (scatter: linear). Behind an ablation flag, prove it re-ranks one mispriced wide/narrow-N reduction matmul toward the device-faster split and leaves the banked min-cores/shared-weight picks unchanged.

BET 3 · THE RING WIN · flash-paged

Flash LSE ring-fold merge (neighbor reduce-scatter of (m,l,O))

hybrid needs-primitive intel HIGH · prod MED

Where the topology physics earns its keep. Once Lk is split across cores, merge the per-core flash partials with a neighbor reduce-scatter (uniform p→p+1, ~130 GB/s measured) instead of the linear fold-to-one that piles the O payload on one link (~36 GB/s floor). Associative+commutative monoid → Rabenseifner valid. Co-bundles into one SDSC bundle by construction (CountedLoop), dodging the cross-bundle wall. Honest: the spill-kill credit belongs to bet 1.

First step

After bet 1 lands, extend _propagate_tiled_reduction_op so Lk is a cross-core split; emit the (m,l,O) combine as a neighbor-ordered LX fold and diff its device-ms against linear gather-to-one on the prefill shape to size the ring-specific increment.

BET 4 · NEW DECODE AXIS · decode

GQA KV multicast-forward to the query-head cohort

hybrid promising-unproven intel MED · prod MED

Distinct lever on the GQA head-group axis (not the banked reduction-axis all-gather). Granite H_kv=8 / H_q=32 → each KV head is shared by 4 query heads. In the 1-head-per-core decode split those 4 cores are a multicast cohort: read the head once, forward on the fast ring, save 3 of 4 redundant HBM KV reads at the decode bandwidth bottleneck. The 32768-slot paged cache can't be pinned — so this is not residency.

First step

Cheap pure-inductor probe: remove the expand+contiguous restickify in the GQA lowering (test_attn_k.py) and confirm the K/V share survives to QK^T; separately check whether decode work_div actually emits 1-head-per-core. Both prerequisites, both answerable with no deeptools work.

BET 5 · REUSABLE FOLD · matmul

Neighbor reduce-scatter for cross-core reduction fold

hybrid promising-unproven intel MED · prod LOW

The reusable primitive bet 3 depends on: replace gather-all-K-partials-to-one with a nearest-neighbor reduce-scatter for any cross-core reduction (split-K matmul, flash @V sum). Moves the accumulate off the 36 GB/s scatter floor onto the 130 GB/s band. Primitives already exist (STCDPOpLx move + per-hop SFP add), so not primitive-first for correctness — but payoff scales with shard count, marginal at split-K's banked 2–4-way home.

First step

On the banked 1.7× measured split-K decode path, measure the fold-tax (device-ms of the linear (k−1) accumulate vs the extra 2(P−1) neighbor hops) to confirm whether the crossover ever favors the ring at real shard counts — cheap measurement, no new codegen.

What we killed upstream

Six mechanisms did not survive the specific device killers — not resurrected

Weight-multicast for M-split
The banked shared-weight unit-BMM fix at 73% util IS the load-once-distribute mechanism it rediscovers — self-defeating.
Split-K partial-sum ring all-reduce
Device-NEGATIVE on prefill SwiGLU +19% measured; latency-bound at M=1 decode where step-count, not bandwidth, caps.
Ring-pipelined paged handoff
Paged has no temporal loop — reduces to residency across a seam.
Page→core channel-affinity placement
HBM is one flat memId=−1 space with no bank affinity; runtime block fragmentation floors real serving at 36 GB/s.
Multicast-Q GQA
M=1 Q payload ~1 KB — below the win floor.
Speculative-decode amortizer
Needs out-of-scope serving infrastructure.

The bar: "just pin the tensors to LX" is the trivial residency baseline and is not an answer. Every surviving mechanism adds a ring-aware algorithmic layer on top — uniform-shift speed, multicast forwarding, neighbor reduce-scatter, hop-distance, or ring-pipelining across the cross-bundle seam. If a proposal reduces to residency, it fails.
